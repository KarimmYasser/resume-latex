# Project Guidelines - LaTeX Resume & Job Applications

- **Always read `resume-content.md` first**: `resume-content.md` is the single source of truth and master context reservoir containing all up-to-date work experiences, projects, technical skills, coursework, honors, and personal details.
- When generating, updating, or tailoring any resume, cover letter, or application material, always consult `resume-content.md` before choosing experiences, metrics, or project details.
- Ensure all tailored resumes strictly adhere to a clean 1-page layout without overflowing onto a second page.
- **Never zero out or crush the spacing between section headers/rules and body text** to save space; rely on `resume.tex`'s enforced `\titleformat{\section}` and `\titlespacing{\section}` and adjust bullet conciseness or font size instead.
- Keep the compilation workflow aligned with `compile_all_tailored.sh` and repository conventions.
